PROJECT OVERVIEW

What does the catalogue tell us?

Explore the balance of movies and TV shows, where titles are associated, and how genres and release patterns vary. Fifteen business questions turn a single table into a structured view of the catalogue.

The raw dataset stays intact. Missing information, multi-value fields and suspicious ratings are handled explicitly during analysis.
